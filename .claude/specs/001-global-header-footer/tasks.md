## Manual visual checklist (for the user; no browser is used by the agents)
1. Announcement bar: grey band above the header on home, collection, product and a page. Two messages rotate every 5 s, pause on hover and when tabbing onto the arrows, and do not rotate with OS reduced motion. The linked message is underlined and clickable; the plain one is not. The arrows sit about 360px either side of centre on desktop and near the edges on mobile. Delete one message: the arrows disappear. Delete both: no strip remains.
2. Header: logo left, menu right of it, icons right in the order account (gold), search, (wishlist when enabled), cart. Thin grey line under the header. It hides on scroll-down and reappears on scroll-up; the announcement bar scrolls away. Add to cart: the bubble appears and updates without a reload. Mobile: hamburger opens a drawer with every menu item.
3. Wishlist: in the header settings, turn on "Show wishlist icon" and set a link. The heart appears between search and cart at the same size, with no count.
4. Footer on home (desktop): all three rows open, sub-group headings with 4-column link grids, then the logo + "Follow Us On" + icons row, then the centred copyright. On collection, product and a page: all rows closed, and each opens with click, Enter and Space with the chevron rotating. Mobile: rows closed, links in 2 columns, the brand row stacked.
5. View source on a collection page: the footer links are in the HTML while the rows are closed.
6. Footer with no social URLs: "Follow Us On" is hidden too.
7. Back to top: appears bottom-right after one screen of scrolling and returns to the top (instantly with reduced motion). The next Tab press lands on "Skip to content". It does not cover the sticky add-to-cart bar on the PDP.
8. Floating cart: add the "Floating cart button" section to any template. On mobile it appears bottom-right with back-to-top stacked above it. Turn on "Show on desktop": it appears at the right edge, vertically centred. Clicking opens the cart drawer, and the count matches the header.
9. Product, collection, cart, search, 404 and home body sections look unchanged (004 intact). The PDP accordions look the same.
10. Merchant content to enter: the store logo and logo height; the main menu; menus for Shop by Category, Featured Collections, Editorial, Others and Purposeful Picks (assign them to the footer menu blocks); the brand name in "Top Searched on …"; the copyright company line; real Facebook/Instagram/YouTube profile URLs; announcement messages and links; the wishlist app page URL (when the app is installed).
